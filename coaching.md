# Coaching sessions 

I offer coaching and mentoring in the following areas: 

- Launching and running your own **not-for profit**
- **Diversity and inclusion** considerations in global teams. 
- Non-traditional research careers (including research software engineering and "late" research degrees).
- Codes of conduct - creating them, commmon mistakes, handling a current crisis (confidentially). 

Historically I have offered coaching in other areas, but I'm feeling a bit out of touch these days: 

- Getting started or finding a safe space in Open Source Software. 

## Practicalities 

- Usually sessions happen over Zoom (or some other virtual platform); I'm in the Cambridge UK area if you would like an in-person session. 
- Sessions are "pay what you like" and use a robin hood fee structure. 
    - If you live in a high-income region and/or have a high stable income yourself, my suggested fee is £150 GBP / hour, or a bundle of ten coaching sessions for £1,200 GBP. 
    - If you have lower income (or none!), I'm happy to set up coaching for free, as long as I have capacity. 
    - If you're able to pay (or your employer is), please pay as much as you can. The higher my paid coaching income, the more easily I can say "yes" to those who can't afford to pay. I can provide an invoice at any desired rate.
